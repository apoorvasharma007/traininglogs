- The exercise picker: typing an exercise's name searches the library and your own exercises,
  closest match first and forgiving of typos. The keyboard's Enter picks the top match, never the
  typed text, and the list fits above the keyboard. When nothing matches, "Custom" adds one.
- Custom exercises pick their muscles on a body, front and back side by side: a tap on a group
  whose muscles share a job (chest, calves) marks the whole group, other groups ask which muscle.
  Picked muscles show as pills that come off with one tap. Secondary muscles, equipment, joint
  movements and movement patterns are optional; compound or isolation is worked out from the joint
  movements.
